# Hydraulic Press Preventive Maintenance Checklist

## Overview

The hydraulic press at Riverbend Packaging is a 50-ton downstroking press used to form and bale packaging scrap and to press fit components during repairs. This checklist defines the preventive maintenance tasks, intervals, and acceptance criteria that keep the press safe and reliable.

## Specifications

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Capacity | 50 tons |
| System pressure | 2,000 psi maximum |
| Reservoir | 40 gallons, ISO VG 46 hydraulic oil |
| Motor | 15 hp, 460 V |
| Stroke | 24 inches |
| Filter | 10 micron return line filter |

## Before Any Maintenance

> **WARNING:** The press stores energy in the hydraulic system and in the raised ram. Lock out MD-8 and hydraulic valve HV-1, bleed accumulator pressure to zero, and install the mechanical ram block before placing any part of the body under the ram.

Follow the Lockout/Tagout Procedure and wear leather gloves, safety glasses, and hearing protection.

## Daily Checklist

1. Check the oil level in the sight glass; it should be between the marks.
2. Inspect hoses and fittings for leaks or weeping.
3. Check the return filter indicator.
4. Test the two-hand controls and emergency stop.
5. Look for oil on the floor and clean it up immediately.
6. Confirm guards and light curtains are in place and functional.

## Weekly Checklist

1. Check system pressure against the gauge and the setpoint of 1,800 psi.
2. Lubricate guide posts with way oil.
3. Inspect electrical enclosures for moisture or loose wires.
4. Verify that the ram block stored on the press is intact.
5. Record cycle count.

## Monthly and Longer Intervals

| Interval | Task | Acceptance criteria |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly | Test pressure relief valve | Opens at 2,000 psi plus or minus 50 |
| Monthly | Inspect cylinder rod for scoring | No visible scoring or pitting |
| Quarterly | Sample hydraulic oil for contamination | Cleanliness ISO 18/16/13 or better |
| Quarterly | Replace return filter element | Element changed regardless of indicator |
| Semi-annual | Inspect hoses for cracking | Replace if cracked or bulging |
| Annual | Replace oil if sample fails | Drain and refill 40 gallons |
| Annual | Inspect press frame welds | No cracks |
| Annual | Calibrate pressure gauge | Within 2 percent of reference |

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Likely cause | Corrective action |
|---|---|---|
| Slow ram speed | Worn pump or low oil | Check oil; test pump flow |
| Press will not build pressure | Relief valve stuck open | Clean or replace relief valve |
| Oil overheating | Low oil or blocked cooler | Check level; clean cooler |
| Oil leak at cylinder | Worn rod seal | Replace seal kit |
| Ram drifts down | Leaking valve or seal | Test and replace valve |
| Excessive noise | Air in system or cavitation | Bleed system; check suction |

## Oil Changes and Disposal

1. Lock out the press and install the ram block.
2. Drain the reservoir into a covered container.
3. Clean the tank and replace the suction strainer.
4. Refill with new ISO VG 46 oil through a filter cart.
5. Bleed air by cycling the ram without load.
6. Dispose of used oil through the approved waste vendor.

Hydraulic oil spills create a slip hazard and must be cleaned up with oil-only absorbent.

## Records

Technicians initial each task on the checklist and record anomalies. Out-of-tolerance findings are escalated to the Maintenance Supervisor before the press is returned to service. Maintain checklists for two years.
