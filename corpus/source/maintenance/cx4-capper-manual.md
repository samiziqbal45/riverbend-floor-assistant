# CX-4 Capper Maintenance Manual

## Overview

The CX-4 is a four-head rotary capper that applies screw caps to bottles on the Riverbend Packaging filling line. Each head uses a magnetic clutch to limit applied torque. Maintenance personnel calibrate the clutches, change over the heads for new cap sizes, and perform routine upkeep as described in this manual.

## Specifications

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Model | CX-4 |
| Heads | 4, magnetic clutch |
| Speed | Up to 120 bottles per minute |
| Cap sizes | 28 mm to 38 mm |
| Torque range (clutch) | 5 to 40 in-lb |
| Air supply | 80 psi, filtered and dry |

## Torque Head Calibration

Torque heads must be calibrated at every changeover and after any head repair. Use a calibrated digital torque tester with a current certificate.

### Calibration Procedure

1. Stop the capper and perform lockout/tagout on disconnect MD-7 and air valve AV-2.
2. Remove the guard panel above the heads.
3. Mount the torque tester adapter on the first head.
4. Restore power temporarily using the maintenance procedure and cycle the head against the tester.
5. Adjust the clutch collar until the tester reads within the target range below.
6. Record the reading for each head on the calibration sheet.
7. Repeat for all four heads. Spread between heads must not exceed 1.5 in-lb.
8. Replace guard panels and remove locks.

### Calibration Targets

Set the heads to **15 to 20 in-lb** for 38 mm standard closures on the 500 mL bottle. Record the setting on the calibration sheet and tag the head with the date.

| Cap size | Bottle | Calibration setting |
|---|---|---|
| 38 mm | 500 mL | 15 to 20 in-lb |
| 28 mm | 250 mL | 10 to 14 in-lb |
| 33 mm | 1 L | 14 to 18 in-lb |

> **NOTE:** If a head drifts more than 2 in-lb between calibrations, replace the clutch spring.

## Changeover

1. Run remaining product through the line and stop the capper.
2. Lock out electrical and pneumatic energy.
3. Remove the chuck inserts and star wheel for the old size.
4. Install the matching chuck inserts, star wheel, and chute guides for the new cap size.
5. Adjust the capper height using the hand wheel so the chuck touches the cap top without pressing.
6. Perform torque calibration for the new size.
7. Remove locks, restore power, and run ten test bottles.
8. Check cap application visually and release the line to Quality for first-article inspection.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Likely cause | Corrective action |
|---|---|---|
| Cap cocked or crossed | Chuck insert worn or height misadjusted | Replace insert; reset capper height |
| Cap torque drifting | Worn clutch spring | Replace spring; recalibrate |
| Caps missing at infeed | Chute obstruction | Clear chute after lockout |
| Head will not rotate | Pneumatic fault or seized bearing | Check air pressure; inspect bearing |
| Scuffed caps | Chuck contamination | Clean chuck inserts |
| Bottle spinning during capping | Worn neck grippers | Replace grippers |

## Preventive Maintenance

| Interval | Task |
|---|---|
| Daily | Wipe chuck inserts; check air pressure |
| Weekly | Lubricate head bearings with food-grade grease |
| Monthly | Inspect clutch springs and grippers |
| Quarterly | Verify torque calibration against certified tester |
| Annually | Rebuild heads; certify torque tester |

## Lubrication

Use only NSF H1 food-grade grease. Over-greasing contaminates caps and bottles. Apply two pumps to each zerk fitting and wipe excess.

## Safety Notes

Guard panels must be in place during operation. Do not reach into the star wheel area while the capper is energized. Follow the Lockout/Tagout Procedure for any servicing inside the guarded area.

## Documentation

Calibration sheets are retained in the maintenance binder for one year and copies provided to Quality on request. Report any calibration out-of-tolerance finding to the Maintenance Supervisor.
