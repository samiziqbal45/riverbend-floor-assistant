# Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) Matrix by Task

## Purpose

This document defines the minimum personal protective equipment required for common tasks at Riverbend Packaging. PPE is the last line of defense after engineering and administrative controls. It does not replace lockout/tagout, guarding, or ventilation. The matrix sets minimums; a task hazard assessment may require more.

## General Floor Requirements

Anyone entering the production floor must wear safety glasses with side shields, steel- or composite-toe footwear, and hearing protection in posted areas above 85 dBA. Long hair must be tied back and loose jewelry removed near rotating equipment. Visitors follow the same rules and must be escorted.

## PPE Matrix

| Task | Eyes/Face | Hands | Body | Respiratory | Hearing |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| General line operation | Safety glasses | None required | Standard uniform | None | Plugs in posted areas |
| Conveyor jam clearing (after LOTO) | Safety glasses | Cut-resistant gloves (A4) | Standard uniform | None | Plugs |
| Capper changeover (CX-4) | Safety glasses | Cut-resistant gloves (A4) | Standard uniform | None | Plugs |
| Sanitizer changeover / CIP | Splash goggles and face shield | Nitrile gloves, 15 mil minimum | Chemical apron | None if ventilated | Plugs |
| Chemical spill response (small) | Splash goggles | Nitrile gloves, 15 mil | Chemical apron, boot covers | Half-mask with organic vapor cartridge if odor present | None |
| RP-200 pump seal replacement | Splash goggles | Nitrile over cut-resistant liner | Apron | None | Plugs |
| Hydraulic press maintenance | Safety glasses | Leather work gloves | Long sleeves | None | Muffs |
| Welding or hot work | Welding helmet shade 10 | Welding gloves | FR jacket | Fume respirator | Plugs |

> **NOTE:** Cut-resistant gloves must not be worn near exposed rotating shafts, rollers, or nip points. Remove gloves if the task places hands near a pinch hazard that could entangle fabric.

## Eye and Face Protection

Safety glasses must meet ANSI Z87.1. Splash goggles are required whenever liquid could reach the eyes, including sanitizer dilution, cleaning solutions, and hydraulic fluid work. Use a face shield over goggles when pouring concentrate or when pressurized lines are opened. Prescription glasses do not substitute for safety eyewear unless rated.

## Hand Protection

Selecting the wrong glove is a common cause of injury.

1. Match the glove to the hazard: cut, chemical, heat, or abrasion.
2. Inspect gloves for tears or pinholes before every use.
3. Replace nitrile gloves after any splash or when swollen or sticky.
4. Wash hands after removing gloves.

Chemical glove selection follows the Safety Data Sheet for the product. Peracetic acid sanitizer requires gloves rated for oxidizers; thin disposable gloves are not acceptable for concentrate handling.

## Respiratory Protection

Respirators are required only when engineering controls cannot hold exposure below limits. Wearers must be medically cleared, fit-tested annually, and clean-shaven where the seal contacts the face. Cartridges are replaced at the end of each shift of use or when odor is noticed.

## Hearing Protection

Areas marked with blue signage exceed 85 dBA. The capper and hydraulic press bay regularly exceed 90 dBA. Foam plugs must be rolled and inserted deeply; muffs are required in the press bay. Dual protection (plugs plus muffs) is required if exposure exceeds 100 dBA.

## Inspection, Care, and Replacement

Employees inspect PPE before each use. Damaged, contaminated beyond cleaning, or expired items are removed from service and replaced at no cost through the supply cage. Face shields and goggles are cleaned with mild soap after each chemical task. Footwear is replaced when the toe cap is exposed or soles are worn.

## Training

Employees receive PPE training at hire and when tasks change. Training covers when PPE is required, what is necessary, how to put on and remove it, its limitations, and proper care and disposal. Supervisors verify compliance during daily walk-throughs and document corrections.
