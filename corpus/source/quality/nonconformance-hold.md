# Nonconformance and Hold Procedure

## Purpose and Scope

This procedure defines how Riverbend Packaging identifies, segregates, evaluates, and dispositions product, material, or packaging that does not meet requirements. It prevents nonconforming product from reaching customers and ensures every event is documented and investigated.

## Definitions

- **Nonconformance (NC):** Any product, material, or process result that fails to meet a specification or procedure.
- **Hold:** The physical and system segregation of suspect product until disposition.
- **Disposition:** The documented decision on what happens to held product: release, rework, sort, scrap, or return to supplier.

## Roles

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Operator | Identify and report; apply hold tags |
| Line Lead | Stop the line when required; segregate product |
| Quality Engineer | Investigate; decide disposition |
| Plant Manager | Approve scrap above cost threshold |
| Maintenance Supervisor | Correct equipment causes |

## Initiating a Hold

1. Stop the line if the nonconformance is ongoing.
2. Notify the line lead and Quality immediately.
3. Identify the extent: determine the last known good check and the first suspect time.
4. Segregate all product between those two points, including pallets, partial pallets, and product in process.
5. Apply a red HOLD tag showing product, lot number, date, quantity, reason, and your name.
6. Move product to the designated hold area or mark it in place if moving is unsafe.
7. Enter the hold in the quality system and open an NC report.

> **NOTE:** When in doubt, hold. Releasing held product is easy; recalling shipped product is not.

## NC Report Contents

An NC report records the product and lot, quantity, specification violated, measured values, date and time, detection method, containment taken, and suspected cause.

## Investigation

1. Review the process records around the event.
2. Interview operators and maintenance.
3. Identify the root cause using 5 Whys or fishbone analysis.
4. Determine whether other lots could be affected.
5. Define corrective and preventive actions with owners and due dates.

## Disposition Options

| Option | When used | Approval |
|---|---|---|
| Release | Product meets requirements after retest | Quality Engineer |
| Rework | Defect can be corrected and re-inspected | Quality Engineer |
| Sort | Defect can be found by 100 percent inspection | Quality Engineer |
| Scrap | Cannot be corrected or sorted | Quality Engineer; Plant Manager over cost limit |
| Return to supplier | Incoming material defect | Quality Engineer and Purchasing |

Reworked product must be reinspected at an increased sample rate and traced to the original NC number.

## Common Hold Triggers

| Trigger | Typical scope of hold |
|---|---|
| Cap torque outside action limits | Product since the last good torque check |
| Fill weight below lower spec | Product since the last in-control check |
| Glass breakage on the line | Product near the breakage area |
| Chemical contact with product | All exposed product |
| Metal detector reject | Product since last good test |

## Closing the NC

The NC is closed when the disposition is complete, the corrective action is verified effective, and records are filed. Quality reports open NCs older than 30 days at the weekly quality meeting.

## Records

NC reports are retained for three years. Trends, such as repeated torque holds or repeated fill-weight holds, are reviewed monthly and fed into continuous improvement projects. Documentation conflicts discovered during investigations are reported to the document owners for correction.

## Escalation and Communication

Any nonconformance that could affect food safety, regulatory compliance, or shipped product is escalated to the Plant Manager within one hour. The Quality Engineer decides whether customers must be notified and documents the decision. Shift supervisors brief the next shift on all open holds so that held product is not moved or shipped by mistake. Held product is never used for sampling, trials, or testing without a documented Quality approval, and hold tags may only be removed by the Quality Engineer or a designee.
